Built from your program

The crew reads your text.
Not a summary of it.

A program loads its own patient-care guidelines. Hiero arranges them for a phone, keeps every line tied to the page it came from, and opens them in a moment, with or without a signal.

The program’s own protocols

Protocols appear in the order and wording the program chose, with the revision on every page, so the crew knows which version they are reading. No paging through a PDF with gloves on.

Doses from those protocols, sized to the patient

Enter weight and age once. Hiero works out the doses the protocol already states, shows the line they came from, and respects the protocol’s maximums. It never supplies a dose the protocol does not contain.

Vent and device references at the bedside

Ventilator settings, device checklists and the program’s reference cards sit next to the protocols that call for them, and they are stored on the phone for the back of an aircraft.

Weight- and age-aware

Move the weight.
Watch the line follow.

Every dose Hiero shows starts as a sentence in your protocol. Hiero does the arithmetic, applies the protocol’s ceiling, and keeps the source in view so the crew can check it at a glance.

When a patient falls under the program’s pediatric rule, the whole app changes color, so nobody reads an adult page by mistake. Try the Pediatric switch.

What Hiero is not

It shows the protocol.
It does not become one.

Not a replacement for medical direction

Hiero does not replace the medical director’s protocol. It shows that protocol, as written, and points back to the page it came from.

Not a decision-maker

Hiero does not choose a destination, a launch, a landing or a dose. Those decisions stay with the crew, the pilot and medical direction.

Not a medical device

Hiero is not a medical device, and nothing on this page is a claim of HIPAA compliance.

Questions

Asked by crews and medical directors.

Does Hiero work without a signal?
Yes. The program’s protocols, dosing, references, oxygen planner, hospital list and the device guides a program adds are stored on the phone. Live layers such as Flight Ops weather and traffic need a connection.
Who controls the clinical content?
The program. Hiero shows the guidelines the program loads and activates. A new or changed protocol does not reach crews until the program has reviewed it, and each page shows its revision.
Does Hiero invent doses or fill gaps in a protocol?
No. Hiero calculates only from doses written in the program’s protocol and applies that protocol’s maximums. If the protocol is silent, Hiero says the program has no matching text.
What patient information does a crew enter?
Age, weight and height drive the arithmetic. Hiero is built so crews do not need to enter names or other identifiers to use it.
Which phones does it run on?
Android and iPhone, with the same program content on both.
